# Length as numbers

"3 bullets," "under 200 words." Adjectives are
guesses; numbers are contracts.
- 🔰 "Keep the toast to 2 minutes — not 'short,' an actual number I can plan around."
- 🏗️ "Cap the changelog entry at 3 bullets — not a paragraph."
